Phantom Chess — Official Game Rules

Version: 0.1
Status: Initial rulebook draft
Project: Phantom Chess

1. Game Overview

Phantom Chess is a chess variant based on traditional chess, with hidden piece identities, customizable formations, and private player notes.

The objective remains to checkmate the opponent's King.

2. The Chessboard and Pieces

- The game uses a traditional 8×8 chessboard.
- Each player has a King and the usual set of chess pieces.
- The King remains visually identifiable.
- All other pieces belonging to the opponent share an identical concealed design.
- The concealed design initially uses a star symbol.

3. Hidden Identity Mechanic

- A player's non-King piece identities are hidden from their opponent.
- The identities become concealed from the opponent once the formation stage begins.
- Concealment remains active throughout the match.
- Players must use observation, deduction, and their private notes to track suspected identities.
- The identities are revealed when the match ends.

4. Custom Formations

- Players can create custom back-rank formations.
- Seven non-King pieces can be arranged in a customizable formation, alongside the traditional King and pawn arrangement.
- Players can save custom formations in formation slots for future matches.
- The formation must follow the agreed setup rules.
- The opponent must not see the actual identities of concealed pieces.

5. Colour Assignment

- Player colours are assigned randomly.
- Colour assignment takes place as part of match preparation.

6. Private Player Notebook

- Each player has a private notebook for recording observations and suspected identities.
- Notes are manually maintained by the player.
- Notes can include piece identity suspicions and last-known positions.
- The notebook is not visible to the opponent.
- Players can use structured notes and mutually agreed note-setting arrangements.
- Movement highlights and capture markers can support the note-taking system.
- Notes do not change the actual positions or identities of pieces.

7. Movement and Chess Legality

- Traditional chess movement rules apply.
- All moves must be legally validated.
- Standard chess special moves are retained, including castling, en passant, and pawn promotion.
- The King must remain subject to standard chess safety rules.
- Hidden identities do not change the actual movement rules of a piece.

8. Captures

- Captures follow traditional chess rules.
- Captured pieces are displayed outside the capturing player's side.
- Captured pieces remain concealed during the match.
- Their actual identities are revealed when the match ends.

9. Check and Checkmate

- Standard chess check rules apply.
- A King in check must be legally protected.
- Checkmate results in an immediate victory for the attacking player.
- The hidden identity mechanic does not override standard King safety rules.

10. Draw Conditions

Traditional chess draw conditions apply:

- Stalemate.
- Threefold repetition.
- Fifty-move rule.
- Insufficient material.
- Draw by agreement between players.

11. Resignation

- A player can choose to resign.
- A confirmation prompt appears with two options: Keep Playing or Resign.
- Confirming resignation ends the match.
- The concealed piece identities are revealed.
- The player is returned to the Home screen after the match-ending sequence.

12. End of Match and Identity Reveal

The identity reveal occurs after:

- Checkmate.
- Stalemate.
- A draw.
- Resignation.

At the end of the match:

1. The identities of all remaining concealed pieces are revealed.
2. The identities of captured pieces are also revealed.
3. The match-ending sequence is completed.
4. The player can exit to the Home screen.

13. Match Preparation

- Players complete the agreed match preparation process before gameplay.
- Colour assignment is random.
- Players configure their formations.
- Private note arrangements are handled through the mutually agreed preparation process.
- Players can cancel the preparation process before the match begins.

14. Initial Testing Rules

- No initial testing time limit is imposed.
- The first version focuses on validating the core gameplay mechanics.
- A points system and traditional move-history log are not part of the initial design.

15. Future Development

Potential future additions include:

- Bot opponents with different difficulty levels.
- Player accounts.
- Online multiplayer and matchmaking.
- Mobile applications.
- Additional game modes.

These are development plans, not rules of the current game.
