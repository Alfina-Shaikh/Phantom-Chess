1. Introduction

Phantom Chess is an original chess variant built around hidden piece identities, strategic uncertainty, and player-maintained private notes.

The game retains the familiar foundation of traditional chess while introducing additional strategic elements that require players to observe, remember, predict, and make decisions with incomplete information.

The objective is to create a playable digital chess experience that offers a different approach to traditional chess without removing its fundamental strategic nature.

2. Core Concept

The defining mechanic of Phantom Chess is hidden piece identity.

Players cannot freely identify the exact identities of their opponent's hidden pieces simply by looking at the board.

This introduces uncertainty into piece recognition and creates opportunities for deduction and strategic planning.

3. Main Features

3.1 Hidden Piece Identity

- Opponent piece identities are visually indistinguishable.
- Players must use observation and deduction to understand the opponent's positions.
- Traditional chess movement and king-safety rules remain fundamental.

3.2 Custom Back-Rank Arrangement

- Players can arrange their own back-rank pieces.
- This introduces an additional preparation and strategy stage before gameplay.
- The arrangement must respect the game's finalized rules.

3.3 Private Player Notebook

- Players may optionally use a private notebook during a match.
- The notebook helps players record suspected piece identities and their last known positions.
- Notes are manually maintained by the player.
- The notebook does not automatically correct or update incorrect assumptions.

3.4 Matchmaking

- Players are matched and assigned colours before match configuration.
- Notebook preferences are resolved before back-rank arrangement.
- Both players proceed through the agreed match settings before the game begins.

4. Traditional Chess Foundation

Phantom Chess retains the following traditional chess principles:

- Legal piece movement.
- Check detection.
- Checkmate.
- King safety.
- Legal move validation.
- Standard chess victory and draw principles, subject to finalized variant rules.

5. Development Vision

Phantom Chess will initially be developed as a responsive website.

Planned development stages:

1. Documentation and concept development.
2. Interface design.
3. Functional website prototype.
4. Testing and refinement.
5. Computer-controlled opponents.
6. Player accounts and online multiplayer.
7. Mobile applications and broader cross-platform availability.

6. Project Philosophy

The objective is not to recreate an ordinary chess game.

The objective is to create a distinct chess experience using familiar chess foundations and original mechanics.

The project will prioritize:

- Clear and consistent rules.
- Fair gameplay.
- Strategic depth.
- Intuitive user experience.
- Proper documentation.
- Gradual development and testing.
